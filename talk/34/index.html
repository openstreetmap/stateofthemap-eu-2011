Open Visit

Artem Dudarev

Details about the talk:
I have mentored students during Google Summer of Code for Openstreetmap for three summers. I will talk a little about the projects and share the mentoring experience. The last summer project is growing into an open check-in application that allows to check-in into various Openstreetmap features. We are building a badge system that encourages data contributions useful for the community. Some other gamification ideas are considered to make "describing the world" more fun.

Details about the speaker:
Artem lives in and maps his native city Donetsk, Ukraine. He organized the first OSM mapping party in former Soviet Union and was a mentor for several Google Summer of Code projects for OSM.

Artem studied Applied Physics in Russia and the United States, worked in Germany. Currenly, he is working on several location based services.
