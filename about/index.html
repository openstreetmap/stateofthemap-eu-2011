About this copy of the website

This is a re-created historical copy of the original State of the Map Europe 2011 website (sotm-eu.org), rebuilt from the Internet Archive’s Wayback Machine and hosted by the OpenStreetMap Foundation. The organisations and addresses named in the original Impressum below are no longer responsible for this site; please direct enquiries and corrections to the project repository on GitHub: github.com/openstreetmap/stateofthemap-eu-2011.

The original Impressum is retained below, struck through, for the historical record (archived original).

State Of The Map Europe 2011 conference is organised jointly by OpenStreetMap Austria and the Research Group Cartography, Institute for Geoinformation and Cartography, Vienna University of Technology.
