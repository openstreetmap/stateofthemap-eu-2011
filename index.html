Recordings

During the conference, all presentations are recorded if the speaker gives his/her permission. You will find these recordings on the Schedule page (little camera icons next to the title of the presentation).

The recordings are facilitated through the support of the Teaching Support Center of the Vienna University of Technology.

Silver Sponsor: Tupalo

Happy to announce Tupalo.com as a Silver sponsor for SotM-Europe!

Tupalo.com is a local search community where close to 4 million people every month discover, review and share the best local businesses around the World, such as Restaurants in Vienna and Beauty Salons in Rotterdam. As long-time fans of the OpenStreetMap initiative, we have integrated OSM local data into our listings pages and are working on a way to feed the collaborative efforts of the Tupalo.com community back into the OSM data set. Founded in 2007, Tupalo.com is live in 5 EU countries and is headquartered in Vienna, Austria, with additional operations in Rotterdam and Warsaw.

– Mike Borras, Tupalo.com

Silver Sponsor: iosphere

Happy to announce iosphere as our next Silver sponsor for SotM-Europe.

iosphere GmbH is the development team behind the popular OffMaps 2 which combines OpenStreetMap with Wikipedia articles and restaurant recommendations for a unique travel guide on the iPhone, iPod and iPad. The app is the first among several apps powered by the company's own QuartzMaps vector rendering framework. QuartzMaps is a native Apple iOS map framework and can be licensed for use in other apps. The company also creates software and apps for reputed clients such as MySwitzerland.com and offers consulting services around OSM.

– Felix Lamouroux, iosphere GmbH

Keynote: Muki Haklay

After Steve Coast last week, we are very pleased to confirm the second keynote speaker for the European OpenStreetMap conference (SotM-Europe) in Vienna: Muki Haklay, researcher at UCL London. Muki is no stranger to the OSM community: If you attended former SotM-conferences you probably know him and his exciting research on OSM data quality as well as the characteristics of mappers.

This year we would like to establish a special focus on research with a relation to OpenStreetMap. Muki will make the perfect connection between the OSM community and the research world with his talk "Observing from afar or joining the action: OSM and GIScience research". We are looking forward to his keynote!

If you haven't registered yet, make sure to get your ticket now!
